Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 15-minute seat hold work?

When you select seats and proceed to booking, our reservation engine places an atomic lock on your specific seat rows. No other traveler can book those seats for 15 minutes while you input passenger information and complete payment.

What is the standard baggage allowance?

Domestic flights include 20 kg checked luggage and 7 kg hand baggage. Regional flights to Dubai, Bangkok, and Singapore provide 30 kg checked luggage. Additional baggage can be purchased under Extras during booking.

Can I cancel or reschedule my ticket?

Tickets can be cancelled or rescheduled directly through Manage Booking up to 24 hours prior to departure in accordance with the specific airline fare rules.
