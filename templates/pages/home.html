Travel Information & FAQ

Important passenger guidance, check-in requirements, and baggage allowances

Airport Check-In Timing

Domestic check-in opens 2 hours before departure and closes 45 minutes prior. International departures close counter check-in 60 minutes before takeoff.

Baggage Allowance Policy

Default allowances include 23 kg checked baggage on domestic routes and 40 kg on international routes, plus 7 kg cabin hand luggage.

Valid Travel Documents

Domestic travel requires a valid NID or Passport. International routes require a Passport valid at least 6 months beyond travel dates.
