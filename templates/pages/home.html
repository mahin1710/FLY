Travel Information & FAQ

Important passenger guidance, check-in requirements, and baggage allowances

Airport Check-In Timing

For domestic flights within Bangladesh, counter check-in opens 2 hours before departure and closes 45 minutes prior. For international departures, please arrive at least 3 hours before scheduled departure.

Baggage Allowance Policy

Standard Economy tickets include 20kg checked baggage for domestic routes and 25–30kg for regional flights. Hand luggage is limited to 7kg (one cabin bag plus one laptop bag or handbag).

Valid Travel Documents

Domestic travel requires a valid National ID (NID), Passport, Student ID, or Driving License. International routes require a Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond travel dates and appropriate destination visas.

Changes & Cancellation

Flight changes and refund requests are processed according to the airline fare rules shown during booking review. Manage and track changes anytime via our Manage Booking portal.
